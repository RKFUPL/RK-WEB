INAARA Serif – Original Font Design Specification

This is an original luxury display serif inspired by high-fashion
typography. It is NOT a copy of any existing commercial font.

Style

-   High-contrast serif
-   Tall condensed proportions
-   Sharp wedge serifs
-   Thin hairlines
-   Crossbar-less triangular A
-   Signature sweeping R leg
-   Elegant fashion/editorial appearance

Character Set

Uppercase: ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ

Lowercase: abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz

Numbers: 0123456789

Punctuation: .,:;!?’“()-–—_/\&@#%+=*

Suggested Metrics

Cap Height: 700 x-Height: 500 Ascender: 760 Descender: -240 Em Size:
1000

Glyph Notes

A - No horizontal crossbar. - Inner counter formed by a graceful curved
stroke.

R - Large circular bowl. - Long sweeping leg beginning inside the bowl.

N - Thin diagonal connecting heavy vertical stems.

O - High contrast oval.

Serifs - Fine wedge serifs with delicate terminals.

Spacing - Optical spacing. - Tight luxury-brand kerning.

Recommended Kerning Pairs: AV AW AY AT LA LT LY TA To Te Tr VA WA YA RA
RO RN

This specification can be used as a blueprint to build an original OTF
font in FontForge, Glyphs, FontLab, or BirdFont.
